## What you can do without paying

- Read, audit, and build the source (it is always source-visible).
- Modify it for internal use.
- Evaluate, test, benchmark, or prototype — including in pre-production
  environments.
- Redistribute unmodified copies under the same license.

## What requires a seat license

- **Production use** of any kind (internal production deployments included).
- **Commercial use** of any kind.
- Providing obscura-based functionality to third parties as a product,
  hosted service, or embedded component.

## Why this model

The owner chose BUSL-1.1 to keep obscura open and auditable while funding its
development through production seats, and to prevent closed forks and
competing services built from the code. Every version still reaches true open
source (Apache-2.0) four years after release.
